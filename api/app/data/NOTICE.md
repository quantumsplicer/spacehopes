# Sources of blocklist.txt

* **censor-text/profanity-list**, English and Hindi lists, released into the public domain (Unlicense).
  https://github.com/censor-text/profanity-list
* **LDNOOBW: List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words**, English and Hindi lists,
  licensed CC BY 4.0. https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words
* Devanagari and Tamil-script entries, Tanglish (romanised Tamil) entries and threat phrases were added by hand
  for this project (they are not copied from a third-party list).
